Sea or pool water?

Read this before you do anything

🚫
Do not turn it on to check if it works. That is what kills most water-damaged phones. If there is moisture inside and you send power through it, you short the components.
🍚
Rice does not work. It does not pull water out of the phone, and it pushes dust and starch into the connectors. Skip it.
✅
Do this instead: switch it off immediately, dry the outside, remove the SIM, stand it upright and bring it to us as soon as you can. Salt water and chlorine are far more corrosive than tap water and act within hours, not days.
